## Required evaluation before semantic outputs

1. Record exact module/lens, stable mounting height/angle, image orientation, room geometry, emissivity assumptions, software/model/dataset hashes, sensor calibration health, complete-frame timestamps/sequence, read time and discontinuities. Current mounting geometry remains unverified.
2. Split by subject, recording session and room. Keep adjacent frames and windows in the same split; reserve unseen D110 household/office sessions as target tests. Report cross-lens and cross-room performance separately.
3. Person detection: precision/recall/F1 and false positives/hour against heaters, sunlight, pets, occlusion, multiple people and warm-room low contrast. Posture: per-class confusion matrix plus explicit unknown/occluded outcomes. No image aspect ratio alone establishes posture.
4. Falls: timestamped transitions, sensitivity, false alerts/day, detection latency and missed transitions across sampling gaps. Do not ask a user to perform real falls. Use safe staged transitions and appropriately collected/labeled data; clinical/general deployment remains a separate validation gate.
5. Immobility: track continuity, identity ambiguity, exits/reentries and duration under quiet/sleeping/occluded scenes. A stationary warm region is only a feature, not an immobile human. Motion/presence fusion needs verified time alignment and ownership; no fusion or alert change is enabled here.
6. Temperature: apparent surface temperatures depend on geometry, emissivity and ambient conditions. No fever/core-body estimate or medical threshold is enabled. Secondary anomaly models require reference/calibration and object/person context.
7. Benchmark compiled inference and capture on the actual classic ESP32 with MQTT/BLE/Wi-Fi load. Reject windows on errors, dropped/duplicate timestamps, reboot, occlusion, stale calibration or ambiguous identities. Check peak heap/stack and sustained throughput, not only average inference time.

Raw images remain in RAM. No customer history is collected for training by this work. Any future opt-in dataset capture must be explicit, bounded, labeled, protected and separately reviewed. Existing heatmap/live-view APIs and PR #22's readiness contract remain authoritative for cloud transport.
